# SAHAAYA LEARN – Inclusive Learning Platform

SAHAAYA LEARN is an inclusive educational web platform specifically designed for three categories of learners:
1. **Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Students** (Indian Sign Language - ISL)
2. **Students with Speech Disabilities** (Augmentative and Alternative Communication - AAC)
3. **Students with Low Vision or Blurred Vision** (High Contrast, Tactile SVGs, Web Speech Read-Aloud)

---

## 🌟 Key Features & 10 Pages

- **Page 1: Home Page**: Inclusive introduction, 3 visible categories, learning methods breakdown, Start Learning CTA, and WCAG 2.1 AAA accessibility features.
- **Page 2: Student Registration & Profile**: Multi-select learning requirements, age, preferred languages (English, Hindi, Tamil, etc.), avatar selection, and personalized dashboard recommendations.
- **Page 3: Student Dashboard**: Individual learner metrics, "Continue Learning" resume banner, completed lessons list, category progress bars (% for ISL, AAC, Low Vision), and quiz score logs.
- **Page 4: Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing Hub (ISL)**:
  - **Module A**: 26 authentic two-handed ISL alphabets (A–Z), Numbers 0–20+ touchpoints, greetings, vocabulary, step-by-step hand position guides, and repeat-and-practice mode.
  - **Module B**: Real-life dialogues (Classroom, Water, Hospital, Shopping, Transport).
  - **Module C**: Visual academic lessons in English, Mathematics, EVS, and Life Skills with synchronized captions.
- **Page 5: Speech Disability Hub (AAC)**:
  - **Module A**: 57 Fitzgerald Key color-coded picture cards across 8 categories (Needs, Food/Water, Emotions, Health, Activities, School, Family, Emergency).
  - **Sentence Builder Ribbon**: Assemble multi-card sentences (e.g. `[I] + [WANT] + [WATER] + [PLEASE]`) with instant Web Speech text-to-speech audio.
  - **Module B**: Scenarios for expressing discomfort, hunger, thirst, and asking for help without forced verbal speech.
  - **Module C**: Non-verbal interactive academics.
- **Page 6: Low Vision & Blurred Vision Hub**:
  - **Module A**: Accessible toolbar with font scaling (100% to 225%), Yellow-on-Black theme, Dark mode, Warm/Sepia, and dyslexia-friendly font.
  - **Module B**: Tactile high-contrast vector diagrams with full audio scene descriptions for Geometry, Plant Lifecycle, Solar System, and Orientation & Mobility.
- **Page 7: Comprehensive Lesson Page**: Structured 9-part pedagogical flow with objectives, explanations, video demos, interactive activities, and end-of-lesson evaluation.
- **Page 8: Practice & Assessment**: Sign identification, number recognition, picture sentence builder checks, matching activities, and audio-supported questions with immediate feedback.
- **Page 9: Progress & Achievements**: XP tracking, milestone badges, and printable/downloadable Level 1, 2, and 3 Certificates of Achievement.
- **Page 10: About & Help**: Mission, problems addressed, FAQs, and contact/accessibility feedback form.
